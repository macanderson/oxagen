Card surface with composable parts. `glow` highlights the featured card; `gradientRing` adds the nebula hairline.

```jsx
<Card glow>
  <CardHeader>
    <CardTitle>Engineering workspace</CardTitle>
    <CardDescription>4 connected sources · 18.2k edges</CardDescription>
  </CardHeader>
  <CardBody><Button variant="gradient" size="sm">Open</Button></CardBody>
</Card>
```

- Parts: `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CardDescription`, `CardBody`, `CardFooter`.
- `glow` = violet focus ring · `gradientRing` = nebula hairline border · `interactive` = hover-lift.
