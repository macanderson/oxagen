The platform — mission control

Mission control for every agent you run

The same map and rules power the web app, the API, and your editor's tools — so the whole team and every agent work from one governed place.

Approved before it runs

Decide who and what can take each action. Anything outside the rules simply doesn't run — no exceptions to chase later.

Measured as it runs

Every model call and tool action is counted, so you always know what your agents cost — down to the team, the task, and the customer you bill.

On the record after it runs

A tamper-evident log of every action, ready for your next security review — instead of a scramble before it.

One shared map

Connect your sources once. Answers come back grounded in your own data, with citations you can open — in the app, over the API, or in your tools.

How it works

Three moves. No rewiring.

STEP 01

Connect

Point Oxagen at your code and the tools your team already uses. It builds one connected, living map of how everything relates.

STEP 02

Ask

Point your agents at Oxagen. They answer from your map — right the first time, with sources you can check.

STEP 03

Govern

Set the rules once. Every action any agent takes is approved up front, measured as it happens, and kept on the record.
