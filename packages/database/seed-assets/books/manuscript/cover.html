A field manual · Second edition

Engineering Deterministic AI Coding Agents

A language model is a probabilistic engine. Almost everything around it can be decided in code: what it retrieves, what stays in its window, which step runs next, what it may do, what it may spend, and what gets recorded. Parts 1 to 13 build that system, with published evidence in each part. Parts 14 to 20 show how to operate the agents you now run. Every part ends with steps for this week and metrics to track.
