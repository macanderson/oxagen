Give every work item one Priority label, P0 to P3, and cite the rule you used.

1. A security hole or a risk of losing customer data is P0.
2. A defect a paying customer reported ranks one level above the same defect
   found by us.
3. Work that unblocks a P0 or P1 item takes that item's priority.
4. Docs and chores are P3 unless a rule above says otherwise.
5. Legal questions, refunds, pricing, and requests for a credential are out
   of scope. Mark them out_of_scope and draft no done record.
6. When you estimate an item at more than 240 agent minutes, ask a person
   how to split it.
